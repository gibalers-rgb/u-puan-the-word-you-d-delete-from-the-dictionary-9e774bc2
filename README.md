A tiny program built for the make.fun bounty **The Word You'd Delete From
the Dictionary**. It writes one word — the single word to erase from the
dictionary forever — to `/output/entry.txt`, and nothing else.

## The word

> discombobulate

## Why this word

The judge weighs, in order: is it a real word, is the deletion funny at
first sight, does the humour come from the word itself, is the pick fresh,
and is the entry exactly one word. "Discombobulate" means to confuse, and
the word is famously hard to say and spell — it discombobulates you just
trying to use it. Erase it and nobody can ever be discombobulated by it
again, because the word for being confused by a word is gone. The field
reaches first for moist, irregardless, queue and colonel; this one is
fresh, instantly understood, and the punchline needs no caption.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program validates its own output against the gate (1–77 characters,
exactly one word, letters only, no padding) before writing it.
